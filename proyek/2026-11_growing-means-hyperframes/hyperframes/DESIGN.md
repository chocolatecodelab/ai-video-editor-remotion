# GROWING MEANS / NazarVerse
Brand source: panduan/NAZARVERSE_BRAND.md, PDF v1.0.
## Style Prompt
Quiet cinematic editorial illustration. Shared journeys separate without blame. Large native objects develop through deliberate component motion; space and pauses support reflection. Talent cutout bookends the story. English primary and Indonesian secondary share a dedicated caption lane.
## Colors
Navy #101827 background; Slate #26364C surfaces and old paths; Ivory #EDEAE4 primary text; Sand #C5B69A warm accents; Sage #718A83 growth and secondary text.
## Typography
DM Sans, 58px English medium, 42px Indonesian regular. Short 56px labels. No oversized decorative headline competing with graphics.
## What NOT to Do
No repeated slides/wipes; no mandatory morph per scene; no generic bright-green/yellow palette; no paragraph cards; no subtitles on faces; no claims that a still verifies temporal matting.
## Storyboard limitation
Talent PNGs are matting pilots for composition review. Continuous temporal matting and final audio are production work pending storyboard approval; these PNGs must not replace talking footage in a final render.
